Kite Clash privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Kite Clash is a kite-flying and kite-fighting game for Android made by Vajra Games. This page explains what information the game handles and how. The short version: there is no account or sign-in, your progress stays on your own phone, ads are shown by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play.

Information the game stores on your device

Your coins, gems, best scores, unlocked kites, outfits and countries, daily rewards and missions, Pecha and Lohri Cup progress, the name you type for the leaderboard, your chosen avatar, and your sound and vibration settings are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to show ads: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example, for an extra life or an extra try), and now and then a short full-screen ad after a lost game. Players who have bought Remove Ads, are in the Kite Club, or have bought anything else in the game do not see the full-screen ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location, and may use it in line with Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Purchases

Kite Clash sells optional items such as kites, outfits, gem and coin packs, Remove Ads, passes and the Kite Club monthly subscription. All payments are made and processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Google Play tells the game only which item was bought, so the game can give it to you and keep it after a reinstall. You can manage or cancel the Kite Club subscription at any time in the Google Play Store app (Profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions). Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game reminds you about daily rewards, festivals and the Lohri Cup. These reminders are set up on your phone itself; no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

Sharing

When you tap a Share or Challenge button, the game opens WhatsApp or your phone's share menu with a ready-made message (for example, your score). Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and the game does not send your data to our own servers.

Children

Kite Clash is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If the game adds features that handle data differently, such as online matches against other players, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
